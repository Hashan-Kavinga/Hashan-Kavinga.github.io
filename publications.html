Selected Research Projects

Selected work spanning computational imaging, 3D sensing, and experimental system development.

Research project Under review

SPARC: Scene-Guided Photon Acquisition and Sparse Representation for SP-LiDAR Compression

Designed an acquisition-aware compression framework that uses RGB scene priors to guide photon sampling, sparsifies retained measurements, and jointly reconstructs dense depth and reflectivity. Evaluated on simulated driving scenes, real measurements, and an NVIDIA Jetson AGX Xavier.

  • Computational imaging
  • 3D sensing
  • Embedded ML

Research project Under review

SPLiDER: Joint Depth and Reflectivity Estimation for Low-Flux Dynamic Scenes

Developed a physics-grounded cross-modal network that processes raw photon timestamp streams to estimate depth and reflectivity in dynamic scenes at fewer than one photon per pixel per frame. It reduced depth error by more than 30% against the strongest baseline across tested regimes.

  • Photon-limited imaging
  • Computer vision
  • Deep learning

Experimental system

Single-Photon LiDAR Lab-Bench Construction

Built a time-correlated single-photon counting testbed with pulsed illumination, SPAD detection, and precision scanning optics to collect realistic raw data for noise characterization, reconstruction, compression, and machine-learning evaluation.
